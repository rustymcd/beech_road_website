2. Information We Collect

When you request an estimate, schedule a service, or otherwise communicate with us, we may collect:

  • Your name, mailing address, phone number, and email address
  • Details about the service or project you're requesting
  • Payment and billing information necessary to invoice you
  • Service history and communication records

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Schedule and perform the services you request
  • Send estimates, invoices, and payment reminders
  • Communicate with you about appointments and job status, including by text message (SMS)
  • Respond to questions and provide customer support

4. Text Messaging (SMS)

If you provide your mobile phone number to us, you may receive text messages related to appointment reminders, estimates, invoices, and job updates. Message frequency varies depending on your project. Message and data rates may apply.

We do not sell or share your mobile phone number with third parties for marketing purposes.

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. You can reply HELP for assistance, or contact us directly using the information in Section 1.

5. How We Store and Protect Your Information

We take reasonable, industry-standard precautions to protect your personal information, including restricting access to customer data to only those who need it to perform our services, and using secure, reputable software platforms for scheduling, invoicing, and communication.

6. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate our business — for example, payment processors (such as Venmo, Cash App, Zelle, or PayPal) and scheduling/invoicing software — solely for the purpose of completing the services you've requested. We do not share your mobile number with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

7. Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to review, update, or request deletion of the personal information we hold about you, using the contact information in Section 1.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
